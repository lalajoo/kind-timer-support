Kind Timer

Work, rest, repeat. No ads.

Help and contact

Set your times and start. Kind Timer is a simple interval timer that alternates exercise and rest, or running and walking.

• Choose activity time, recovery time and repetitions • Save your favorite settings as reusable timers • See remaining time and the next interval in large, clear text • Hear guidance while the screen is locked • Choose female or male voices in 11 languages, or simple tones • Automatically keep completed and stopped sessions • Browse compact history and delete selected entries • All features without accounts, ads or in-app purchases

Settings and history stay on your device and may be lost if you uninstall the app. Every voice cue is included, so no internet or separate voice download is needed. Check media volume and the timer status after calls or disconnecting headphones. Force-closing the app stops guidance.

Privacy Policy

2026-11-02 · MINSUK SONG

Kind Timer does not collect or transmit personal information, usage logs or advertising identifiers to a developer server. There are no accounts, ads or analytics trackers. Timer settings, history, language and review-prompt counters are stored on your device. You can delete selected history in the app; uninstalling may remove local data. Operating-system backups depend on your device settings.

Reviews are optional and handled by Apple or Google. The app does not track whether you reviewed it or which rating you chose. If you email support, your address and message are used only to respond. Please do not send sensitive personal information.

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process access information for security and operations. We have not added advertising or analytics tools to this website.
